NeoOta Platform

Japanese Media Intelligence & Data Infrastructure

NeoOta is a media intelligence platform focused on Japanese entertainment and creative industries. The platform is being developed to aggregate, structure, and connect information about Japanese media, works, creators, and related industry activity.

Core Platform Areas:

  • Data Ingestion & Infrastructure: Building systems for collecting and processing information from Japanese-language media sources.
  • Data Structuring & Relationships: Organizing information around works, creators, companies, releases, events, and their relationships.
  • Media Intelligence & Discovery: Developing tools for following relevant developments, discovering related works and creators, and making fragmented media information easier to understand.

U.S. Development | Japan-focused operations planned 2027
